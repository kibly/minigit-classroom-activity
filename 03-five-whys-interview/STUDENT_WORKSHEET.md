# Activity 3 Student Worksheet

## Command prompts

Conduct interviews for at least two prompts:

- “I want `status`.”
- “I want `diff`.”
- “I want to `add` a file.”
- “I want to `commit` my changes.”
- “I want to view the `log`.”
